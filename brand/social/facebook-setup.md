# Facebook Page setup — RateMyPlace

Walkthrough for setting up the RateMyPlace Facebook Page from a logged-in personal account. Copy blocks are below each field. UI labels match Facebook as of May 2026 and may drift if FB redesigns.

> **Reminder:** RateMyPlace launches as a general renters platform. Do not mention any specific city anywhere on the Page. Voice and visual rules per [`brand.md`](../../brand.md).

---

## Before you start

- Logged in to Facebook with your personal account (the personal account becomes the Page admin; this is invisible to visitors).
- Final logo mark for the profile photo. Check whether `brand/social/ig-pfp.png` exists and is current per `brand.md` v1.3. If not, regenerate before this step or skip the profile photo and add it later.
- Cover photo (optional for launch). If you don't have one yet, skip and add to to-do at the bottom.

---

## 1. Open the page creator

Go to `facebook.com/pages/create` or click **Pages** in the left sidebar, then **+ Create Page**.

When prompted "Which option is best for you?":

- Select **Public Page** (not "Upgrade your existing profile")
- Click **Next**

---

## 2. Page name

Paste:

```
RateMyPlace
```

---

## 3. Category

Start typing in the Category field and pick from FB's autocomplete. You can add up to three:

1. Public Service
2. News & Media Website
3. Community

If "Public Service" doesn't surface, try "Public Service & Government" or "Cause." The signal you're going for: civic resource, not business or storefront.

---

## 4. Bio / Short Description (255 char max)

```
A public record of rental housing, from the people who know it best. Verified tenant reviews, weighted by the health and safety research that actually affects renters' lives. Know before you sign.
```

Click **Create Page**.

Facebook drops you onto the new Page surface. Everything below happens from there.

---

## 5. Profile photo

Click the camera icon over the empty profile photo circle.

Use the RateMyPlace mark on a Signal Teal `#0F766E` background per `brand.md` §10 (mark centered, 1080×1080, reverse-mark variant since the amber lit window pops most against teal).

If `brand/social/ig-pfp.png` exists and is current, upload that. If not, regenerate it first or skip this step.

---

## 6. Cover photo

Click the camera icon on the cover photo area.

Dimensions: 1640×624 (FB displays at 820×312, use 2x for retina).

Recommended treatment per `brand.md` §10 + §11:

- Paper `#F6F4EE` background
- "Know before you sign." in Instrument Serif, centered or left-aligned
- Full lockup (`brand/logo-lockup.svg`) top-left
- Hairline rule near the bottom with `ratemyplace.org` set as a tracked uppercase eyebrow

If this asset doesn't exist yet, skip and add to the to-do at the bottom of this file. The page will still launch fine without a cover.

---

## 7. About → Edit details

Click **About** in the Page's left nav, then **Edit details** (or similar — FB sometimes labels it "Page info").

**Website**

```
https://ratemyplace.org
```

**Email**

```
meredith@ratemyplace.org
```

**Phone:** leave blank

**Address:** leave blank

**Hours:** "Always open" or skip. The Page is a website, not a storefront.

**About / long description** (often labeled "Additional information" or "Bio"):

```
RateMyPlace is a public record of rental housing, from the people who know it best.

We publish verified tenant reviews of rental buildings and landlords, weighted by the health and safety factors that actually affect renters' lives. Our scoring is grounded in peer-reviewed housing research, not vibes.

Renters can't afford to be picky. They can't get honest answers from listings. And until now, they had nowhere to check.

Know before you sign.

ratemyplace.org
```

Save.

---

## 8. Username (URL handle)

Settings → Page Setup → Username.

Try in this order, take whichever is available:

1. `ratemyplace`
2. `ratemyplaceorg`
3. `ratemyplaceapp`

Avoid anything with a city. Match the IG handle if/when that account is created.

---

## 9. Call-to-action button

On the Page, click **Add action button** (sometimes appears as **Edit action button** if FB pre-populated one).

- Action: **Learn More**
- Link: `https://ratemyplace.org`

Skip "Send Message" unless you want to monitor an FB inbox. Skip "Sign Up" — too transactional for a public record.

---

## 10. First post

Click **Create post** (the compose box at the top of the Page feed). Paste:

```
RateMyPlace is live.

A public record of rental housing, from the people who know it best. Verified tenant reviews, scored by the health and safety factors that actually affect renters' lives.

If you've rented, you can write a review. The next tenant will read it.

Know before you sign. → ratemyplace.org
```

Publish. Then click the three-dot menu on the post and select **Pin to top of page**.

---

## 11. Final verification pass

Open the Page in an incognito/private window (so you see what a stranger sees, not what an admin sees) and check:

- Page name reads "RateMyPlace"
- Zero mentions of any city anywhere on the Page
- Website link works and points to `https://ratemyplace.org`
- Profile photo and cover render correctly at the FB-cropped sizes
- First post is pinned at the top
- CTA button is "Learn More" and points to `ratemyplace.org`
- The "Page Transparency" section (scroll down on the Page) shows just the date created and country, no admin names
